### What can be tested

- source image, face is small but clear
- source image with harsh shadows
- source image in blue lighting
- source image with underexpose or over exposed lighting
- source image with heavy makeup
- source image with face tattoo
- source image with facial features such as beard
- source image with accesories - bare minimum eyes should be blocked but can be tested still too
  - glasses
  - mask
- Overlapping faces
  - Like one face in front of the other
- partial faces
- like with angles
  - facing up
  - facing down
  - to the left
  - to the right

### What can't be tested

- blurry photos, with a blurry face - resolution unknown
  - ie) small faces can be detected given that it isnt to blurry
