## Insight Face (API)

- is open source, free under the MIT license for commercial and academic usage
- It does it by detecting face, swap major landmarks/eyes, jaws and etc, align both faces to same orientation
- The runtime to get the face should be 250 or a little more ms, but the response time to feel instant needs to be 100ms or less, so this would be put on hold, unless no other options show up.
- InsightFace: The Open-Source Powerhouse for 2D & 3D Face Recognition, Detection and Swapping - BrightCoding

## Face2Face (API)

- Is open source, and free under MIT license
- Identity comparison, identity transfer, multiface swapping. Done by numerical vectors
- 300ms - 500 ms on GPU
- SocAIty/face2face: Swap faces in images and videos. Create face embeddings. Enhance face image quality. Deploy as a web api.
